Sign in

  1. Be at the bar and allow location. Only one DJ at a time.
  2. Sign in with the starting PIN the owner gave you. It asks you to make your own PIN and agree to the Host, DJ & Staff Terms right away.
  3. In the Tips card, save your payment links once. After that, tips turn on by themselves whenever you're the DJ signed in.

During the night

  • Next singer moves the line
  • Pause for breaks
  • Fix name if someone signs up wrong
  • Add a singer for guests without the app: name, song, pick their spot in line. Move to # puts anyone at any spot.
  • Bad photo? Tap it, then 🙈 Blur now. It's off the TV and wheel instantly.
  • Lyrics: on / off in Night controls hides the lyrics buttons on guests' phones.
  • Cast the TV screen or the Spin wheel to the bar TV.
  • While you take tips, everyone with the app gets a "Tip the DJ?" card after each song.

End of night

  • Tap Stop taking tips, then Sign out. Leaving the bar also signs you out.

What's in it

  • Tonight's tips for every DJ, by DJ and by guest, plus the last 30 days.

First sign-in

  1. Sign in on the Queue page with the starting PIN the owner gave you.
  2. Make your own PIN and agree to the Host, DJ & Staff Terms when it asks. Use your PIN from now on.

Start of night

  1. Queue page, tap Open sign-ups
  2. Location check is always on: singers must be at the bar to sign up
  3. Cast the TV screen

Running the night

  • Same controls as the DJ: Next singer, Pause, Fix name, photos, Lyrics on / off.
  • 🔒 Lock tonight or Lock always stops someone adding new photos. Rowdy night? Turn on Approve photos first.
  • DJ left or forgot to sign out? In the Tonight's DJ card tap Close DJ session to close their night. That signs them out and stops their tips, and the next DJ can sign in. Need tips off? Stop tips tonight. Staff never see tip money.
  • Staff can reset a DJ's PIN or remove a login in the Hosts card.
  • Ads page: headline, picture, pick days, Save. Shows at the top of the customer app.

End of night

  • Start a new night. History stays saved.

Owner only · house PIN

  • Add DJs and staff in the Hosts card with a starting PIN. Pick DJ or Staff. They must make their own PIN the first time they sign in.
  • Make DJ / Make staff switches someone's login type.
  • Live view (/watch, your owner list PIN): the lineup and tonight's tips for every DJ, view only.
  • Never share the house PIN. Give everyone their own login. Customers never need a PIN: they use the QR code or the link.
  • Tip money is only in the Bar Owner app (/bar): your owner list PIN, or the own login of any staff you tap Make manager on. Each DJ sees only their own tips on the host page. Staff and the house PIN don't see tips.
